Proposals for the function of the website

1 (Benson): The first and foremost benefactor of our website should be us (group members). In the sense that it teaches us the skills we need to make an application. Related to that, our application could also inform other learners as well. by coincidence all my other ideas are also educational, which I think fits the Computing for the Common Good line of thinking.

2 (Benson): Another proposal I was thinking of was a application that could guide people toward worthwhile media. This is because I am particular about media consumption and wish that others would consume higher quality media. However if I were to be less misanthropic, I believe that improving media literacy would be a net positive for society.

3 (Benson): The last idea I can think of is an rpg or arg sorta thing. It could use some rudimentary bot or the group could just make a bunch of scripted answers. The player would play the game and the 'AI' (actually a scuffed chatbot) would change based on player input. After a short adventure the game would end and then a conclusion would tell the player the positives and negatives of releasing this 'AI' the player influenced. If at all possible I want to avoid making it too moralistic. By the end of a couple playthroughs the player should have a general idea of avoiding the "Paperclip Problem" and other robotics concepts.
